# Launch backlog: first 10 destinations

**Audience:** Guests staying at Casa de la Familia in Cerros del Águila.  
**Status:** Research-backed production queue. These are Launch Day candidates from [launch-candidates.md](launch-candidates.md), not approved place records or publication approvals.  
**Research checked:** 4 October 2026.

## Recommendation

Complete the ten destinations below in the listed order. The set covers two examples of each content type while concentrating on the nearby Mijas/Fuengirola area and on places for which useful official source material is already available. It puts the best-documented local options first, then spends more research time on the larger town visits and the restaurant with incomplete online details.

“Readiness” estimates how much publication work is already supported by the source packet; it is not a quality rating and cannot clear a publication blocker. Effort estimates are **remaining active editorial hours** from the current research packet through evidence completion, drafting, independent review, validation and handoff. They exclude reply delays. All ten still need an exact, dated driving route from Casa to the visitor point, so none is ready for approval or publication.

## Destination work packets

### 1. Restaurante Sheriff

- **Content type:** Restaurant (`Restaurants` category).
- **Source material gathered:** [Official site](https://elsheriff.com/) gives the address (Calle Playa del Chaparral 4, Mijas Costa), phone, beachfront setting, weekly opening pattern and kitchen service windows. [Official menu](https://elsheriff.com/la_carta.cfm) provides current menu and listed prices, including children’s dishes.
- **Still required:** Dated Casa-to-entrance drive and arrival/parking check; confirm current hours and menu directly before publication; booking policy; payment; high chair/stroller practicality; step-free entrance and toilet details; allergy/cross-contamination handling; a transparently calculated family meal example.
- **Remaining effort:** 2–3 hours.
- **Estimated publication readiness:** 73/100.
- **Workflow state:** Identity and initial official source pack gathered; claim verification, draft, independent review, record validation and human decision remain.

### 2. La Cala Beach (Playa de La Cala)

- **Content type:** Beach (`Beaches` category).
- **Source material gathered:** [Mijas Tourism beach profile](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/nature/beaches/la-cala-beach?print=1&tmpl=component) publishes beach dimensions, sand/seabed description, beach restaurants, hammocks, first-aid post, access and adapted bathing. [Mijas 2026 beach-state page](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/nature/beaches/state-of-the-beaches) gives the seasonal lifeguard timetable. [2026 Blue Flag notice](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/news/1211-mijas-beaches-renew-their-five-blue-flags) provides a current municipal flag-status lead.
- **Still required:** Exact access point and dated route; current day-of-visit water/beach status; verify seasonal service dates, parking price/capacity, toilets/showers, shade and access details; clarify what “adapted bathing” includes and its operating dates; family cost assumptions.
- **Remaining effort:** 2.5–3 hours.
- **Estimated publication readiness:** 72/100.
- **Workflow state:** Official profile gathered; exact arrival and current facility/access facts, then draft and review, remain.

### 3. El Bombo Beach

- **Content type:** Beach (`Beaches` category).

[Mijas 2026 beach-state page](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/nature/beaches/state-of-the-beaches) is the seasonal lifeguard source; the [2026 Blue Flag notice](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/news/1211-mijas-beaches-renew-their-five-blue-flags) is a current status lead.

- **Estimated publication readiness:** 74/100.
- **Workflow state:** Official profile gathered; arrival and service verification, drafting and review remain.

### 4. BIOPARC Fuengirola

- **Content type:** Attraction (`Family activities` category).
- **Source material gathered:** [Official FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/) cover typical duration, daily opening pattern, age/ticket rules, supervision, weather, animal visibility, food and broad accessibility. [Official tickets](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/entradas/) give current ticket prices. [Directions and parking](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/how-to-get/) give the address, public transport distances and parking-discount arrangement. See also [current schedules](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/explore-the-park/schedules/) and [official site](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/).
- **Still required:** Dated Casa-to-entrance route; whether advance booking is required or just advisable; actual parking charge after discount; detailed wheelchair route/toilet information; check current food service and ticket conditions. Build an example family total only after confirming party ages and selected ticket basis.
- **Remaining effort:** 2–3 hours.
- **Estimated publication readiness:** 70/100.
- **Workflow state:** Strong official visitor source pack gathered; a few consequential details and dated route, then draft/review/validation, remain.

### 5. Senda Litoral de Mijas: La Cala–El Sheriff open section

- **Content type:** Nature/Hiking (`Nature` category).
- **Source material gathered:** [Mijas Tourism’s 2026 opening notice](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/news/1219-the-section-of-the-coastal-path-linking-la-cala-with-el-sheriff-beach-comes-into-service) says the specific section linking La Cala to El Sheriff was put into service on 1 June 2026 and describes it as a 700 m linear section with 3 m usable width.
- **Still required:** Confirm current open condition and exact endpoints; dated drive to the chosen start; walking time and surface/gradient; safe road crossings, shade, seating, toilets, water, parking and return options. Do not present this as a continuous coast-wide path or assume the return route is identical.
- **Remaining effort:** 2.5–3.5 hours.
- **Estimated publication readiness:** 76/100.
- **Workflow state:** A precise official segment source is gathered; route-level practicality and live status need checking before drafting and review.

### 6. Carromato de Mijas (Museum of Miniatures)

- **Content type:** Attraction (`Family activities` category).
- **Source material gathered:** [Mijas Tourism museum page](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/culture/museums/museum-of-miniatures) gives location, collection size, ticket price, contact details and seasonal hours. [Municipal maps, leaflets and timetables](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/useful-information/maps-leaflets-timetables) appears to give a different closing time for part of the year.

- **Remaining effort:** 2.5–3.5 hours.
- **Estimated publication readiness:** 65/100.
- **Workflow state:** Official identity and useful visitor facts gathered; the hours conflict is a blocker until resolved; then draft, independent review and validation.

- **Remaining effort:** 3–4 hours.
- **Estimated publication readiness:** 60/100.
- **Workflow state:** Destination identity and official orientation sources gathered; route scope and decision-critical details remain before draft/review.

### 9. La Muralla Botanical Route, Mijas Pueblo

- **Content type:** Nature/Hiking (`Nature` category).
- **Source material gathered:** [Mijas Tourism’s English route leaflet (PDF)](https://turismo.mijas.es/images/Guias/RUTA%20BOT%C3%81NICA/folleto%20ruta%20botanica%20INGLES.pdf) describes a circular route of about 1.5 km and one hour, with a more-or-less level, low-difficulty section after Calle Cuesta de La Villa. Its map marks amenities and nearby facilities.

- **Remaining effort:** 3–4 hours.
- **Estimated publication readiness:** 70/100.
- **Workflow state:** Detailed but dated official route source gathered; current field conditions and start-point logistics are needed before drafting and review.

## Production gates for this batch

1. Start with **Restaurante Sheriff, La Cala Beach and BIOPARC** as the first calibration batch. Each has a substantial official source base and tests a different template.
2. Gather and record an exact, dated driving route from Casa for every destination. Route times must point to the named entrance, beach access, trailhead or parking arrival point; no estimates from memory.
3. Resolve blockers before drafting polished copy: the Carromato timetable conflict; current La Muralla route status; the coastal path’s present condition; and current La Familia menu/operating details.
4. Draft each page using its content type, record claim-specific sources/check dates, and complete the independent checklist review and validation gate.
5. Hand eligible records to the human editor-of-record. These readiness scores and this backlog do not approve or publish any destination.

## Batch estimate

The ten work packets represent approximately **26.5–36 active editorial hours** to reach a human approval decision, depending on source responses and route/access findings. This is a planning range, not a calendar promise. The lowest-risk early batch is Sheriff, La Cala and BIOPARC; the remaining seven should proceed independently so one difficult operator confirmation does not hold up the others.
