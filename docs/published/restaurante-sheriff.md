# Restaurante Sheriff

**A long-running, beachfront restaurant serving seafood, Spanish dishes and familiar family options**  
**Address:** Calle Playa del Chaparral 4, Mijas Costa, Málaga  
**Telephone:** +34 952 493 722  
**Last verified:** 4 October 2026

## Quick verdict

Restaurante Sheriff is a useful choice for a sit-down meal by the sea, especially if your group wants a broad menu with seafood, meat, pasta, pizza and children’s dishes. The operator lists service Tuesday to Sunday, with the kitchen open **13:00–16:00 and 19:00–22:00**; it lists Monday as its weekly closing day. [Official opening information](https://elsheriff.com/)

**Before you set out:** the [Mijas Tourism listing](https://turismo.mijas.es/es/gastronomia/donde-comer/chiringuitos/restaurante-sheriff) currently carries the note “Cerrado desde el 28 de febrero” (“Closed since 28 February”), while the restaurant’s own site publishes weekly hours and a reservations page. **Verification required:** call **+34 952 493 722** to confirm that it is open on your chosen date. Do not make the drive based on the posted schedule alone.

**Best for:** a relaxed lunch or dinner when you want a full restaurant menu and are happy to check current details before you go.  
**Choose somewhere else if:** you need confirmed step-free access, a specific allergy protocol, or a quick snack rather than a meal.

## Why visit

The restaurant is on the beachfront and has operated since 1973, according to its own site. Its online menu includes seafood, fish, meat, salads, pasta and children’s meals. [Restaurant website](https://elsheriff.com/) · [Current menu](https://elsheriff.com/la_carta.cfm)

**Our recommendation:** This is a practical family meal option where different ages can order different kinds of food. Menu prices are individually listed, making it easier to estimate the meal before arriving.

## Opening, booking and meal budget

- **Opening:** Tuesday–Sunday from 11:00; kitchen service 13:00–16:00 and 19:00–22:00; closed Monday, according to the operator’s website. Confirm around holidays or seasonal changes.
- **Booking:** The operator provides an [online reservations page](https://elsheriff.com/reservas.cfm), but does not say whether booking is compulsory. Reserve or call **+34 952 493 722**, particularly for a larger party or peak-season meal.
- **Menu examples:** Children’s hamburger €9.80; small spaghetti Bolognese €7.90; children’s pizza €9.30. Adult hamburger €14.00; house salad €10.40; fried squid €12.90. [Official menu](https://elsheriff.com/la_carta.cfm)
- **Example family spend:** Two adult hamburgers and two children’s hamburgers total **€47.60 before drinks or extras**. A meal with seafood, starters or desserts will cost more. Prices are those displayed on the menu when checked and may change.
- **Typical meal duration:** Allow about 1–1.5 hours. This is an editorial planning estimate, not a restaurant service-time promise.

The restaurant is beside the beach, so it can be a pleasant meal to combine with a coastal outing.
