# BIOPARC Fuengirola

**A half-day animal and habitat visit in central Fuengirola**  
**Address:** Calle Camilo José Cela, 6–8, 29640 Fuengirola, Málaga  
**Last verified:** 4 October 2026

## Quick verdict

BIOPARC is a good choice if your family enjoys animal viewing and can spend around three hours walking and taking breaks. It is open every day from 10:00; closing time changes with the season. Tickets are a significant part of the day’s cost, but children under three enter free. Buy online at least 24 hours ahead for the published €1-per-ticket saving. Check the [official schedule](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/explore-the-park/schedules/) before setting out.

**Recommended for:** families who want a substantial, organised outing and are comfortable with animals sometimes being out of view.  
**Choose something else if:** you need a very short or free activity, or your family expects guaranteed close-up sightings of every animal.

## Why visit

The park presents tropical habitats and more than 200 animal species. The visit is self-paced, and BIOPARC says the average visit takes about three hours. Animals may not always be visible: the habitats are designed around animal welfare, and weather or veterinary care can affect which animals are out. [BIOPARC visitor FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

**Our recommendation:** Treat this as a relaxed half-day, not a timed checklist. If a particular animal or activity is the main reason for going, check the day’s [activity schedule](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/explore-the-park/schedules/) first.

## Travel from Casa de la Familia

Allow **about 15–25 minutes by car** from Casa de la Familia in Cerros del Águila to the park entrance in normal traffic. Allow more during busy periods and check live navigation before leaving. [Open driving directions](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Casa%20de%20la%20Familia%2C%20Urbanizaci%C3%B3n%20Cerros%20del%20%C3%81guila%2C%20Mijas&destination=BIOPARC%20Fuengirola%2C%20Calle%20Camilo%20Jos%C3%A9%20Cela%206-8%2C%20Fuengirola&travelmode=driving).

The Fuengirola Cercanías station is about 600 metres from the park; the bus station is about 700 metres away. Confirm your return service before choosing public transport. [Official directions and transport information](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/how-to-get/)

## Time and cost

- **Recommended visit:** Allow 3–4 hours including a break; BIOPARC’s stated average visit is about 3 hours.
- **Current standard day tickets:** €29.50 general (ages 10–64); €23 for children aged 3–9 and visitors over 65; €16 for visitors with a disability; under 3s free.
- **Example family budget:** Two adults and two children aged 3–9 cost **€105** at standard listed prices. Buying online at least 24 hours ahead saves €1 per ticket, making that example **€101**. Food, parking and optional purchases are extra.
- **Tickets:** [Official ticket page](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/entradas/). The ticket allows one entry; re-entry is not included.

Prices and eligibility are those shown on the official site when checked; confirm them at purchase. The park also lists discounts with proof of eligibility. Children aged 10 and older pay the general rate.

## Opening and booking

BIOPARC states that it opens every day at 10:00, with seasonal closing times. The ticket office closes one hour before the park, which is also the latest entry time. No advance reservation is stated as a condition of ordinary day admission. Online purchase at least 24 hours ahead qualifies for the published €1 saving. Check the [live schedule and ticket terms](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/) before travel.

Children under 16 must be accompanied throughout by a responsible adult; one adult may be responsible for up to five minors. Separate documentation rules apply to unaccompanied visitors aged 16–17. See the [official visitor rules](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/) if that applies to your group.

## Is it a good fit for your group?

These are practical recommendations, not guarantees of enjoyment.

| Group | What to consider |
|---|---|
| Toddlers | Under 3s enter free. A stroller is permitted; plan a snack and rest breaks during the roughly three-hour visit. |
| Children | Often a good fit for children interested in animals. Ages 3–9 use the child ticket; ages 10+ pay general admission. |
| Teenagers | A good potential fit for animal and habitat enthusiasts. Teens under 16 need an adult; 16–17-year-olds visiting alone have additional paperwork requirements. |
| Adults | Works well as a paced half-day. Admission includes the activities published by the park, subject to any specific activity conditions. |
| Seniors | The park lists a reduced rate for visitors over 65. Ask the venue about any individual mobility needs before booking. |

## Access, strollers and wheelchairs

BIOPARC describes the park as fully accessible and says the visit route accommodates baby strollers. That is the operator’s general statement; it does not provide a detailed route-by-route gradient or surface guide. [FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

**Wheelchair advice:** The operator describes the park as accessible, but detailed information about gradients, rest points and accessible toilet locations was not available in the visitor material reviewed. If those details determine whether your party can visit comfortably, contact BIOPARC on **+34 952 666 301** before you go.

## Parking, toilets and food

- **Parking:** BIOPARC has no car park of its own. Plaza España car park is directly opposite; visitors can request a **50% discount** at the park’s ticket office or shop and apply it when paying at the garage. The final parking price depends on the garage’s current tariff. [Official parking directions](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/how-to-get/)
- **Toilets:** The official visitor map marks toilet facilities, including baby-changing facilities. [Official park map (PDF)](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/MAPA-WEB_INGLES.pdf)
- **Food and drink:** BIOPARC Café serves food and snacks. Outside food is generally not allowed; the park’s rules make exceptions for babies, some children’s snacks and food for people with intolerances. Tell ticket-office staff about an intolerance on arrival. [Official FAQs and rules](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

## Weather and practical tips

The park says it opens year-round and that rain does not automatically prevent a visit. Much of the experience involves moving through habitats, so bring sun protection in warm weather and a light rain layer when the forecast calls for it. Animal visibility can change with weather. Check the schedule on the day, arrive early enough to enjoy the full visit, and remember that leaving the park normally ends your admission for the day.
