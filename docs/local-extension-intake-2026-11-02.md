## Method

- Identity, address, website and position: Google Places API (New) text search, one top hit per query, read 2026-11-02 and checked by hand. Maps establishes identity and position only.
- Drive time: Google Routes API from the project origin (36.5356875, -4.6626875), Saturday 2026-11-07 10:00, predicted traffic. Walking time shown only under 20 minutes (owner rule); none of these is that close.
- Nothing about opening days, hours, prices, access or suitability has been checked. That comes with the record, from official or own sources.
- Dropped from the sweep: 'Pico de Mijas' (Google placed it 45 min away, outside the area; trails are listed instead), 'Calamorro' (covered by the Benalmádena cable car), a duplicate coastal-path hit, and a cooking school listed as temporarily closed (Muna's Kitchen, Fuengirola).

## Structure decisions for the owner

- **Everyday life (group 1)** does not fit the six categories (`Restaurants`, `Beaches`, `Excursions`, `Nature`, `City`, `Family activities`). Options: (a) a separate 'practical information' page outside the place records, (b) a new category approved by the editor-of-record, or (c) only markets as records (e.g. under `City`) and the rest on a practical page.
- **Events (group 5)** are dates, not places. Options: (a) a season calendar page linked from place records, (b) mention events in the relevant town or beach record.
