# Season calendar (draft)

**Status: draft for the owner, 2026-11-02.** Owner decision 8 (`docs/owner-decisions-2026-11-02.md`): events go in a calendar by month, with official dates per year, linked from the relevant places. Guests arrive over a long season, so each month lists what is on. 2027 dates are given only where an official source fixes them or the date follows from the church calendar; the rest say 'dates announced by the council' and must be filled in when published. Text in English like the place records.

| Month | Event | Where | 2027 date | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| January | Cabalgata de Reyes (Three Kings parade) | Fuengirola, Mijas and other towns | Evening of 5 January (eve of Epiphany, 6 January) | Fixed by the church calendar; route and time announced by each council [check] |
| February | Carnaval | Fuengirola, Mijas, Benalmádena | Around Ash Wednesday, 10 February 2027; dates announced by the councils | Ash Wednesday computed from Easter 2027 |
| March | Semana Santa (Holy Week) processions | Fuengirola, Mijas Pueblo, Benalmádena | Palm Sunday 21 March 2027 to Easter Sunday 28 March 2027 | Easter 2027 computed (Gregorian computus); procession times announced locally |
| June | Noche de San Juan (beach bonfires) | Beaches in all towns | Night of 23 to 24 June 2027 | Benalmádena tourism lists 23.06.2027-24.06.2027 [1]; beach rules vary by town |
| June | Feria de San Juan, Arroyo de la Miel | Benalmádena | 24-29 June 2027 | Benalmádena tourism [1] |
| July | Virgen del Carmen | Fuengirola and Los Boliches (also La Cala) | 16 July 2027 (feast day; festivities around it) | Fuengirola council: 16 July is the main day, with mass and procession [2]. A sea procession is not confirmed by the pages read |
| September | Feria de Mijas (Virgen de la Peña) | Mijas Pueblo | Early September; 2027 dates announced by the council | 2026 edition 7-12 September [3] |
| October | Feria del Rosario | Fuengirola fairground | 6-12 October 2027 | Fuengirola tourism: 'siempre se celebra del 6 al 12 de octubre' [4]. Fuengirola's markets do not run on the fairground those weeks (2026 calendar) [5] |
| December | Christmas lights and markets | Fuengirola, Mijas; Málaga's Calle Larios is about 40 min away (outside the area) | December; dates announced by the councils | [check] |
