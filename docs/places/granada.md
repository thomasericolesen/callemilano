The Alhambra's gardens, Generalife and Alcazaba are open daily (08:30-18:00 winter, 08:30-20:00 summer), while the Nasrid Palaces use timed entry capped at 300 visitors per 30-minute slot — a general visit takes around 3 hours. Tickets (General Day Tour €22.27, or the cheaper Andalusian Monuments Visit at €8.48) are sold via the official site, by phone, at ticket machines, or same-day at the office subject to availability; secondary sources consistently (though not officially confirmed) describe tickets as released roughly 3 months ahead and liable to sell out in high season, so advance booking is strongly recommended. Visitors 14 and older without an accompanying adult must carry ID; under-14s must be accompanied. The official site recommends flat, closed footwear for cobblestones, uneven steps and bare-earth sections. Beyond the Alhambra, the Albaicín's free Mirador de San Nicolás viewpoint offers a well-known view back toward the Alhambra with the Sierra Nevada behind it, though it gets crowded, especially at sunset. Parking, full accessibility details and on-site food have not been confirmed from an official source."
