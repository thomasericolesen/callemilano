The Alcázar de Jerez, an 11th-century Moorish fortress, anchors the old town; a camera obscura inside carries an additional fee. Its official opening hours are now confirmed: daily 08:30-14:30 from January to June and October to December, and with longer weekday hours (08:30-17:30) from July to September; current admission price is not confirmed (the only official figure found is a 2020, pre-pandemic document, too old to publish as current). The city also has a flamenco museum (Centro Andaluz de Flamenco) and is the historic home of the Royal Andalusian School of Equestrian Art and the Carthusian horse breed.
