# BIOPARC Fuengirola

**A tropical animal park in central Fuengirola**  
**Visitor address:** C. Camilo José Cela, 6–8, 29640 Fuengirola, Málaga. [Directions and transport](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/how-to-get/)

## Quick verdict

**A strong half-day option for families who enjoy seeing animals and learning about tropical ecosystems.** The operator describes an average visit of about three hours. Admission is paid; children under three enter free. Allow extra time for breaks and animal-viewing stops. [BIOPARC FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

**Editorial recommendation:** Consider it if your group is happy to walk at its own pace and admission fits your budget. Choose something else if your priority is a very short, low-cost outing, or if you need confirmed step-free routes and facilities before deciding; those details are not sufficiently specific in the available accessibility information.

## Why visit

**Verified facts:** BIOPARC describes itself as an animal park focused on biodiversity conservation, with recreated tropical ecosystems and more than 200 species. The operator notes that animals may not be visible at first glance, and that some may be out of view for veterinary or weather-related reasons. [Official BIOPARC site](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/), [FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

**Editorial recommendation:** Go for a paced animal and habitat visit, rather than expecting a quick walk past guaranteed close-up sightings. Check the day's activity schedule if a particular keeper activity matters to your group.

## Who will enjoy it

**Editorial recommendations, not age guarantees:**

- **Toddlers:** Likely to enjoy animal viewing and the children's play area. The operator says strollers are accommodated and children under three enter free. A three-hour average may be a long stretch for some toddlers, so plan breaks.
- **Children (7–12):** A good potential fit for children interested in animals and nature. The operator lists child admission for ages 3–9; ages 10 and up pay the general rate. Under-16s must remain with a responsible adult.
- **Teenagers (13–17):** Best for teens with an interest in animals, habitats, or conservation. Ages 13–15 must be accompanied by an adult. The operator's requirements for 16- and 17-year-olds visiting without an adult should be checked before travel.
- **Adults:** A good fit for adults who enjoy animal parks and are comfortable with a multi-hour visit.
- **Seniors:** Potentially suitable; the operator lists a reduced rate for visitors aged 65 and over and describes the park as fully accessible. Confirm route, seating, and rest options if these affect your decision.

BIOPARC says children under 16 must be accompanied at all times by an adult responsible for them, at a maximum of five minors per responsible adult. [Official FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

## Who should choose something else

**Editorial recommendation:** Consider another activity if your group does not enjoy animal attractions, has only a short window, or needs a low-cost outing. Also choose another destination until details are confirmed if a member of your party needs specific wheelchair routes, accessible toilets, or other access arrangements to be certain of a workable visit. BIOPARC's broad “fully accessible” statement does not describe every route or facility.

## Typical family budget

**Verified standard daily admission checked 4 October 2026:**

- General admission: **€29.50** per person.
- Ages 3–9: **€23**.
- Ages 10 and over: general rate, **€29.50**.
- Under 3: **free**.
- Age 65 and over: **€23**.

Prices are from the [official ticket page](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/entradas/); confirm the current rate and conditions before purchase. The FAQ says tickets bought online at least 24 hours in advance are €1 cheaper; verify that offer at checkout.

**Example totals, admission only:** Two adults and two children aged 3–9: **€105**. Two adults, one child aged 3–9, and one child aged 10 or over: **€111.50**. Two adults and one child aged 3–9, with a child under 3: **€82**. These examples exclude parking, food, and optional activities. The park says the adjacent car park offers a 50% visitor discount, but its underlying price was not verified here; see Parking.

## Parking

**Verified:** BIOPARC has no car park of its own. Plaza España car park is directly opposite, and BIOPARC visitors receive a 50% discount. Ask for validation at the ticket office or shop before paying; payment is made at the car park. [How to get there](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/how-to-get/)

## Weather considerations

**Verified:** BIOPARC says it maintains normal activity on rainy days, although weather or veterinary reasons can affect whether some animals are visible. [FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

**Verification required:** The amount of sheltered walking, shade, and heat exposure across the visitor route was not confirmed. Check the forecast and the park's current notices; do not assume rain or heat will leave every part of the visit unchanged. The operator says tickets are not refunded for weather once a visitor has entered.

## Practical tips

- Check the closing time for your date; the park opens at 10:00, but closing varies, and the ticket office closes an hour earlier.
- Allow roughly three hours inside, then add your drive and any meal or rest break.
- Buy online at least 24 hours ahead if the €1 advance discount is still offered for your ticket type; confirm the price at checkout.
- Children under 16 must stay with a responsible adult. Carry age identification in case the operator requests proof for a ticket rate.
- Ask for the Plaza España parking discount at the ticket office or shop before paying at the car park.
- You normally cannot leave and re-enter on the same ticket. Plan meals and breaks accordingly.
- Do not plan around seeing a particular animal at a particular time unless the operator confirms it; some animals may be out of view.
