# Guest information priority list

**Audience:** Guests staying at Casa de la Familia in Cerros del Águila. This list ranks the information guests need to choose an activity and make a workable plan.

“Can the page be published without it?” means whether CalleMilano should recommend the place publicly without that information. **No** means the answer must be verified or the place held back. **Yes, with disclosure** means state that the detail is unknown or not applicable and point guests to a reliable next step; never imply certainty.

## Critical

Without these answers, a guest may choose the wrong destination, waste a trip, exceed their time or budget, or discover a participation barrier too late.

| Information field | Why the guest needs it | When it is used | Can the page be published without it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Place identity and core experience** | Establishes what the guest is choosing and what they will actually do there. | While browsing, comparing, and deciding whether the outing fits the party. | **No.** Confirm the identity and describe the core experience before recommending it. |
| **Exact visitor point and directions** | Prevents navigation to the wrong entrance, town center, or unrelated pin. | When judging travel feasibility and before leaving. | **No.** A clearly named visitor point and usable directions are required. |
| **One-way drive estimate from Casa de la Familia** | Shows whether the travel commitment fits the day. | During initial filtering and day planning. | **No.** Provide a checked estimate and date. If a reliable estimate cannot be established, do not recommend the place as a ready-to-plan outing. |
| **Typical time at the destination** | Lets guests distinguish a short visit from a half-day or full-day commitment. | When choosing between an outing and combining activities. | **No.** Provide a supported duration or a clearly qualified estimate and its scope. |
| **Current opening status and applicable schedule** | Prevents a wasted trip and helps guests choose a date. | Before selecting a date and again before departure. | **No.** Verify the schedule or clearly direct guests to the official live calendar; do not imply the place is open when that is unknown. |
| **Formal age, height, supervision, and participation restrictions** | A rule may exclude a guest or require adult supervision even if the activity seems suitable. | Before booking and before choosing an activity for a mixed-age party. | **No, when relevant.** Verify applicable restrictions. If the venue has none, say so only when supported; otherwise disclose uncertainty and direct guests to the operator. |
| **Party suitability and physical effort** | Helps guests decide whether the experience is likely to work for their ages, interests, and stamina. | During browsing and comparison. | **No.** Provide evidence-aware guidance and reasons; do not use an unsupported family rating as a substitute. |
| **Access limitations on the core visit** | Steps, terrain, route length, or inaccessible facilities can make a visit impractical. | Before choosing or setting out, especially when mobility or stroller needs apply. | **No, when access is material.** State what is known and what remains uncertain; do not imply access is suitable without evidence. |
| **Current admission price and price basis** | Lets the guest understand the main cost and compare options fairly. | During budget filtering and before booking. | **No, for paid places.** Show checked prices or clearly direct guests to current official pricing. If free entry is verified, state that; do not use an unverified price label. |
| **Required or likely extra costs** | Parking, mandatory transport, or required equipment can change whether the outing fits a budget. | During comparison and before departure. | **No, when material.** Identify known required costs. Optional costs may be described as optional; unknown costs must be disclosed. |
| **Booking requirement and official booking route** | Guests need to know whether the date can be planned and where to confirm a reservation. | Before committing to a date-sensitive outing. | **No, when booking applies.** Give the official booking link and policy. A link is not confirmation of live availability. |
| **Freshness of time-sensitive details** | Guests need to judge whether hours, prices, booking, and access information may have changed. | Before choosing a date and before leaving. | **No.** Show when key details were checked and provide a responsible official source for current confirmation. |

## Important

These details often change the choice or make the outing easier, but a page can still help guests if the information is clearly marked unavailable or not relevant.

| Information field | Why the guest needs it | When it is used | Can the page be published without it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Parking location, cost, restrictions, and walk to entrance** | Helps guests plan arrival, cost, and the route from vehicle to visit. | Before driving, especially with children, mobility needs, or limited time. | **Yes, with disclosure.** State that parking details are unverified and link to an official source when possible. Do not claim nearby parking is at the venue. |
| **Toilets and food availability and location** | Helps families plan breaks and understand whether facilities are on-site or nearby. | When assessing a longer visit or planning meals. | **Yes, with disclosure.** Separate on-site from nearby and identify unknowns. |
| **Public transport and last-mile details** | Allows guests without a car to judge whether the whole journey is practical. | When selecting a destination and planning the return. | **Yes.** If no option is verified, omit the claim. If mentioning transit, link to current schedules and avoid implying service is available without checking. |
| **Seasonal suitability and material weather exposure** | Heat, rain, wind, shade, shelter, or seasonal operation can affect comfort and activities. | When choosing a date or making a backup plan. | **Yes, with disclosure.** State known seasonal limitations; avoid implying year-round suitability when uncertain. |
| **Food type, children’s options, dietary information, and outside-food rules** | Helps guests manage allergies, meal timing, and children’s needs. | Before a long visit or when food needs affect the choice. | **Yes.** Link to an official menu or policy if available; do not infer dietary suitability. |
| **Dog policy and material conditions** | Guests travelling with a dog may need to rule out a place or plan around restricted areas. | During browsing and before departure. | **Yes, with disclosure.** Do not describe a place as dog-friendly without verified policy; direct guests to the operator if uncertain. |
| **Activity type and useful search terms** | Helps guests find relevant experiences without knowing place names. | On the home page and while filtering results. | **Yes, if the place remains clearly described.** A useful activity description can substitute for a missing label. |
| **A concise reason to choose the place** | Makes comparison meaningful and explains what is distinctive about the experience. | While scanning recommendations and deciding which detail page to open. | **Yes, but the place should not be featured prominently without one.** A plain, accurate description is better than promotional wording. |
| **Contact details for unresolved practical questions** | Gives guests a way to confirm a material detail directly. | When access, booking, opening, or policy information is unclear. | **Yes.** Provide an official contact route when available; otherwise link to the responsible venue or authority. |
| **Nearby alternative or backup reason** | Helps guests recover if a place is closed, unsuitable, or weather-affected. | When building a flexible day plan. | **Yes.** Useful but not required for an individual place page. Recommend alternatives only when their fit and logistics are understood. |

## Optional

These may improve browsing or enjoyment but should not delay publishing a useful, well-checked guide.

| Information field | Why the guest needs it | When it is used | Can the page be published without it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Photo gallery** | Helps guests recognize the place and picture the visit. | During browsing and deciding whether to learn more. | **Yes.** Text and directions should remain useful without photos. Use only images with clear reuse permission and accurate captions. |
| **Extended history or background** | Adds context for guests who want to learn more. | After the guest has already decided the place may interest them. | **Yes.** Keep essential planning information ahead of background detail. |
| **Nearby related places** | Helps guests combine activities or compare alternatives. | When planning a full day or adapting to a change. | **Yes.** Include only when proximity and relationship are clear and useful. |
| **Suggested multi-stop itinerary** | Reduces planning work when a guest wants to visit more than one place. | For guests planning a full day or short trip. | **Yes.** A single-place page should stand alone; do not publish an itinerary without realistic durations and travel between stops. |
| **Same-day traffic, weather, or remaining ticket availability** | Can improve decisions close to departure. | On the day of the visit. | **Yes.** The first public version can direct guests to current providers; never present stale estimates as live conditions. |

## Publishing rule of thumb

Publish a place as a recommendation only when its identity, core experience, visitor point, travel and visit-time estimates, material restrictions/access, price basis, and date-sensitive opening or booking guidance are reliable enough for a guest to make a decision. For other details, show what is known, identify what is not confirmed, and provide a useful official next step. If an unknown could make the outing unsuitable or waste the trip, hold the recommendation until it is resolved.
