# Proposed launch candidates

**Audience:** Guests staying at Casa de la Familia in Cerros del Águila.  
**Status:** Editorial shortlist for research and review, not approved place records or publication recommendations.

This shortlist applies the category mix and two research waves in the [pilot content plan](pilot-content-plan.md), and the guest-decision priorities in the [content priority list](content-priority-list.md). It ranks 30 candidates for Launch Day, 12 for Phase 2, and 8 for Phase 3. Phase 2 and Phase 3 split the pilot plan’s second wave to make the rollout more manageable. Category assignments are proposals using the approved category names; any borderline classification must be resolved by the editor-of-record.

“Editorial effort” estimates the work to reach the pilot publication checklist, not the time to write a page: **Low** = clear identity and relatively stable facts; **Medium** = several material claims or scope details to verify; **High** = volatile, seasonal, access-sensitive, distant, or operationally complex. These are initial estimates. A candidate is not ready to publish until its visitor point, dated route estimate from Casa, duration, current operating details, material restrictions, and evidence meet the checklist. A name in this shortlist is not evidence that the destination is open, suitable for every family, or currently offering a particular service.

## Before converting candidates to place records

1. Confirm there are no duplicate identities or overlapping scopes. Parque Fluvial de Fuengirola appears once; keep its nature focus distinct from specific attraction records.
2. Resolve all “scope/category to define” and “operator to confirm” notes with the editor-of-record. Do not create a place record for a generic itinerary or unnamed operator unless the schema and editorial decision support that scope.
3. Screen replacements if a candidate is closed, duplicative, too difficult to verify, or cannot meet the pilot publication checklist. Preserve the target mix as guidance, not a quota.
4. Research and record claim-specific evidence, checked dates, a named visitor point, route estimate from Casa de la Familia, realistic visit duration, material restrictions/access, cost basis, and current official confirmation route.
5. Keep all candidates in draft/review until validation passes and the human editor-of-record approves them. This shortlist itself is not publication approval.
