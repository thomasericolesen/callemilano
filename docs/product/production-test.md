# Production test: ten launch destinations

**Audience:** Guests staying at Casa de la Familia in Cerros del Águila.  
**Research checked:** 4 October 2026.  
**Purpose:** Test whether the five content templates can produce useful, source-supported guest pages from the current launch candidates.

These are production drafts, not publication approvals. Scores are checklist-based estimates of how much publication work is complete. A score of 100 means no material required information is unresolved; **any destination with an open publication blocker must remain unpublished regardless of score**. The clearest shared blocker in this test is a dated route estimate from Casa to the exact named visitor point.

## Attractions

### 1. BIOPARC Fuengirola

**Animal park | C. Camilo José Cela, 6–8, Fuengirola**

**Quick verdict:** A strong half-day candidate for guests interested in animals and tropical habitats. BIOPARC reports an average visit of about three hours; animals may not always be visible at first glance. [Official FAQs](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

**Guest fit:** Children under three enter free; ages 3–9 have a child rate; ages 10 and up pay the general rate. Under-16s must be accompanied by a responsible adult. BIOPARC says it accommodates strollers and is fully accessible, but detailed wheelchair routes and facilities are not described. [Ticket rates](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/entradas/), [access and rules](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/faqs/)

**Plan:** Average on-site visit about 3 hours. Standard admission: €29.50 general, €23 ages 3–9 and 65+, free under 3; two adults and two children ages 3–9 = **€105 before food and parking**. Opens at 10:00 daily; seasonal closing varies. Check the [current schedule](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/explore-the-park/schedules/) and [tickets](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/entradas/). Plaza España car park is opposite, with a 50% visitor discount after validation; actual tariff is not listed in the reviewed source. On-site café; outside-food exceptions are limited. [Parking/directions](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/how-to-get/), [services](https://www.bioparcfuengirola.es/en/prepare-your-visit/information-of-interest/services/)

**Travel from Casa:** **Verification required**—dated route estimate to the entrance not obtained.

**Missing information still required:** Dated drive estimate; confirmation whether advance booking is required; detailed wheelchair route/accessible toilet information; current parking amount and food-service times.

**Publication readiness: 70/100.** Core experience, prices, hours, visit duration, age rules, and main logistics are sourced. Unresolved arrival/access details include a required route estimate.

**Estimated editor time to complete:** 2–3 hours.

### 2. Caminito del Rey

**One-way gorge route | North access, Ardales, to south exit, Álora**

**Quick verdict:** A demanding, pre-booked day outing for fit visitors comfortable with heights. The official route summary gives 8 km and 3–4 hours; the FAQ describes 7.7 km including 4.8 km of access and 2.9 km of boardwalk. The page must state which distance definition it uses. [Route](https://www.caminitodelrey.info/en/your-visit/access-route), [FAQ](https://www.caminitodelrey.info/en/your-visit/faq)

**Guest fit:** Under-eights are not admitted. Visitors aged 8–17 must be accompanied by an adult and carry ID. The authority discourages visitors with vertigo, inadequate physical preparation, and certain heart, respiratory, or locomotor conditions. Wheelchairs, strollers, canes/crutches, pets, and bulky bags are prohibited. [Mandatory rules](https://www.caminitodelrey.info/en/your-visit/mandatory-standards), [prohibitions](https://www.caminitodelrey.info/en/your-visit/prohibitions)

**Plan:** Advance reservation is mandatory. General admission is €10; official guided visit €18. Visitor-centre parking is €2/day; shuttle is €2.50/person. For four using general entry, parking, and shuttle, allow **€52 before food and travel**. Arrive at the access at least 30 minutes early. No toilets along the route; facilities are at entry/exit points. Pack permitted water/snacks and eat before/after the boardwalk. [Tickets](https://www.caminitodelrey.info/en/tickets/buy), [parking/shuttle](https://www.caminitodelrey.info/en/your-visit/parking-shuttle-bus-crv), [recommendations](https://www.caminitodelrey.info/en/your-visit/recommendations)

**Travel from Casa:** **Verification required**—route must be calculated to the chosen north-side parking/arrival point, then matched to the one-way return plan.

**Missing information still required:** Dated drive estimate and chosen parking/return itinerary; reconcile distance wording; check tickets, closures, and shuttle availability for the actual date.

**Publication readiness: 82/100.** Strong official evidence for booking, rules, route, duration, costs, and facilities; exact house route and operational date check remain.

**Estimated editor time to complete:** 2–3 hours.

## Beaches

### 3. La Cala Beach

**Named beach | La Cala de Mijas; Torreón access point for adapted bathing**

**Quick verdict:** A locally relevant beach option with municipal listings for easy access and seasonal services. The municipal profile gives a length of 1,258 m, average width 38 m, six beach restaurants, and one first-aid post. It also lists adapted bathing and Blue Flag status. [Mijas beach profile](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/nature/beaches/la-cala-beach?print=1&tmpl=component)

**Guest fit:** Mijas lists access on foot, by car, and by bus. A 2025/26 municipal update says La Cala has a universal accessibility certification, and the adapted-bathing leaflet describes shaded area, loungers, amphibious chairs, a lift, and seasonal trained assistance. These refer to the serviced zone, not the whole beach. [Accessibility update](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/news/1179-the-mijas-coastline-renews-its-tourism-quality-and-sustainability-certifications), [adapted bathing details](https://turismo.mijas.es/images/playas/INFO%20PLAYAS%20PDF/PLAYAS%20CON%20BA%C3%91O%20ADAPTADO.pdf)

**Plan:** **Editorial recommendation:** allow a half-day if swimming and breaks are intended; duration depends on weather and the party. The municipality lists lifeguard service in 2026 from 11:00–20:00 daily June–September, with limited May/October dates and hours. Check current bathing status and service calendar before leaving. [Beach state 2026](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/nature/beaches/state-of-the-beaches)

**Travel from Casa:** **Verification required**—dated drive estimate to the chosen public access point not obtained. The [La Cala map](https://turismo.mijas.es/images/Guias/PLANO%20LA%20CALA%202024/PLANO%20LA%20CALA%202024%20COMPLETO.pdf) can support final entrance selection.

**Missing information still required:** Exact beach access/parking point, route estimate, parking prices and walk to sand, current toilets/showers and food opening, same-day water status, stroller/wheelchair surface details outside adapted-bathing zone.

**Publication readiness: 72/100.** Municipal sources give unusually useful accessibility and seasonal lifeguard facts; arrival point and changing facilities remain unresolved.

**Estimated editor time to complete:** 2–3 hours.

### 4. Playa de Cabopino (Artola / Cabopino)

**Beach | Marbella, beside the protected Dunas de Artola**

**Quick verdict:** A beach-and-dune outing, but the beach and natural-area access points should be treated as separate visit choices. Andalusia’s tourism authority describes installed footpaths and advises visitors to stay off the dunes to protect their natural balance. [Dunas de Artola](https://en.andalucia.org/listing/dunas-de-artola-o-cabopino/15533101/)

**Guest fit:** Marbella Tourism lists an assisted-bathing service with amphibious chairs, shaded areas, and rails to help water access. It must be arranged in advance; the municipal page lists service hours for June–September. This does not establish a step-free route across all sand or dune paths. [Artola/Cabopino beach details](https://turismo.marbella.es/vive/mar/playas/calahonda-2.html)

**Plan:** **Editorial recommendation:** reserve half a day for beach time; add time for a dune walk. **Verification required:** a supported beach-vs-dune duration, current parking price/lot, toilets, food, lifeguard coverage, and same-day bathing conditions. Nearby marina facilities are not proof of beach facilities. [Cabopino marina](https://turismo.marbella.es/vive/mar/puertos/puerto-de-cabopino.html)

**Travel from Casa:** **Verification required**—choose the exact beach access or dune-route entrance before calculating.

**Missing information still required:** Dated route estimate; exact entrance and parking plan; current public facilities and seasonal beach services; verified access surface and route distance/time; water-safety status on visit date.

**Publication readiness: 54/100.** Strong identity and protected-area guidance, but insufficient practical arrival and facilities information for a decision-ready beach page.

**Estimated editor time to complete:** 3–5 hours.

## Towns

### 5. Mijas Pueblo Historic Centre

**Historic town area | Mijas Pueblo, starting point to be selected**

**Quick verdict:** A close cultural stroll among whitewashed streets, historic buildings, and viewpoints. Mijas Tourism describes the old centre as being on a mountain slope and identifies the old Moorish wall and gardens, the oval bullring, Inmaculada Concepción church, and Virgen de la Peña sanctuary. [Historical centre](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/culture/monuments/historical-center)

**Guest fit:** Best for a party comfortable with hills and wandering rather than a flat, fixed attraction. Specific stroller/wheelchair suitability depends on the route and remains **Verification required**. Paid sites and their hours are separate from walking the town.

**Plan:** **Editorial estimate:** allow 2–3 hours for a compact stroll, excluding museums. The municipal access page lists bus routes 122 from Fuengirola, 112 from Málaga, and 121 from Torremolinos; check the current timetable. It also lists municipal parking, but rates/availability need a current check. [How to get there](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/basic-information/how-to-get-there), [visitor map](https://turismo.mijas.es/images/Guias/PLANO_MIJAS_ESP-ING-FRA-ALE_JUNIO_2025_ok-1.pdf)

**Budget:** No ticket price is stated for a street stroll; featured museums and activities are separately priced and must not be implied as included. **Verification required** for selected stops and parking cost.

**Travel from Casa:** **Verification required**—calculate to the specific chosen parking/start point, not the municipality centroid.

**Missing information still required:** Named circuit and point, dated route estimate, circuit elevation/surface and duration confirmation, current parking fees, toilets/food along route, access-specific route advice, hours/prices for any featured paid landmark.

**Publication readiness: 60/100.** The town’s identity and key sights are supported, but guest-facing scope, route, access, and arrival plan need definition.

**Estimated editor time to complete:** 3–5 hours.

### 6. Marbella Old Town

**Historic centre | Suggested starting point: Plaza de los Naranjos**

## Quick verdict

**A flexible town visit for guests who want historic lanes, squares, churches, and places to eat or browse.** Marbella Tourism describes a historic centre containing Roman, Arab, and Christian traces, the Arab wall, museums, squares, chapels, and the Church of Our Lady of the Incarnation. [Historic centre](https://turismo.marbella.es/vive/cultura/patrimonio-historico/casco-historico.html)

**Plan and fit:** **Editorial recommendation:** allow 2–3 hours for a relaxed walk, longer for museum or church visits. The municipal map identifies Plaza de los Naranjos, the Tourist Office, and other old-town places; this page should cover the historic-centre stroll, not promise access to every landmark. [Marbella Old Town map](https://turismo.marbella.es/images/media/attachments/area-profesional/material-turistico/plano_marbella_puerto_banus_y_casco_antiguo.pdf)

**Access:** Cobblestones are documented at Plaza del Altamirano; route-wide gradients, surfaces, and step-free access are not established. [Plaza del Altamirano](https://turismo.marbella.es/vive/cultura/patrimonio-historico/casco-historico/plaza-del-altamirano.html)

**Travel from Casa:** **Verification required**—dated route to selected parking or drop-off point not obtained. **Budget/opening:** Street exploration has no single ticket price or town-wide opening time; pay entry and hours apply separately to individual venues. Verify parking, toilet access, and current landmark hours before featuring stops.

**Missing information still required:** Dated drive estimate; validated walking circuit and duration; current parking cost and walk-in route; accessible route/toilet details; schedules and admission for any selected sights.

**Publication readiness: 62/100.** Official scope and map support a useful start, but the access route and transport arrival plan remain incomplete.

**Estimated editor time to complete:** 2–4 hours.

**Marked mountain route | Start: Mijas Pueblo viewpoint**

**Guest fit:** Mijas advises planning around physical ability and health, bringing water, food, suitable footwear, a warm layer, sun protection, and checking the forecast. The map's four MIDE values are 1, 2, 2, and 2 for environment, orientation, movement, and effort. A family with young children should assess the climb rather than relying on distance alone. [Trail guidance](https://turismo.mijas.es/en/nature/trekking), [trail map](https://turismo.mijas.es/images/Guias/senderismo.pdf)

**Plan:** Allow at least the mapped walking time plus breaks. The trail network starts at El Mirador de Mijas Pueblo. No ticket/booking is identified by the municipality; current closures, parking cost, water/toilet facilities, and marked-path condition need confirmation. The map is dated 2022, so route condition must be rechecked.

**Travel from Casa:** **Verification required**—dated drive estimate to the viewpoint/trailhead and parking plan not obtained.

**Missing information still required:** Current route status/signage check; dated drive estimate; parking/return logistics; access to trailhead, facilities, seasonal hazards, and confirmation that 2022 route figures remain current.

**Publication readiness: 68/100.** The route has unusually clear official distance, time, and ascent data, but that map is dated and current access/arrival details are missing.

**Estimated editor time to complete:** 2–3 hours.

### Most common missing information

1. **Dated drive estimate from Casa to an exact visitor point:** missing for all 10. It is the common publication blocker.
2. **Arrival specifics:** parking price/location, walk from car to actual start/entrance, and return logistics for linear routes.
3. **Access detail:** broad accessibility statements do not settle step-free route, accessible toilets, stroller surfaces, or wheelchair use across the full visit.
4. **Current operational detail:** restaurant hours/menu/booking, seasonal beach services and same-day bathing status, trail closure/access status, and attraction booking availability.
5. **A properly scoped duration:** strongest for BIOPARC and Caminito; less certain for open-ended town visits and coastal walking.

### Estimated effort to reach 50 launch-ready destinations

The ten-page test averages about **3 editor hours per destination** to close the current gaps and finish review. A straight extrapolation is about **150 hours for 50 destinations**. Allow **150–200 hours (roughly 4–5 editor-weeks)** for the pilot because route checks, direct venue follow-up, seasonal detail, and high-effort candidates vary. This assumes one editor can reuse the page patterns and evidence habits established here; it excludes engineering, photography production, and long delays waiting for replies. Every destination still needs individual source checks and editor approval.
