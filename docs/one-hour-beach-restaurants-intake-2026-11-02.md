# Intake: restaurants near the one-hour beaches (2026-11-02)

Owner decision 13: restaurants next to the six selected beaches. Candidates from Google Places text search around each beach record's point (searches such as 'chiringuito', 'restaurante pescado', 'espetos'), filtered to places Google lists as operational, within about 1.2 km straight line, and well rated with a substantial number of reviews. Ratings and review counts were used only to shortlist and are not recorded. Bars were left out (owner bar rule). Nothing about menus, hours, prices or child-friendliness is checked; the cuisine labels are working labels from Google's place type or the name.

Owner selection page: https://claude.ai/artifact/1uvFqCZBd5LEWGDDS3aVr7 (collection `choices`). Checked 2026-11-02 by Claude.

Correction 2026-11-02: in the first version rE04 and rF05 repeated rE02 (Casa Fernando) and rF01 (La Escollera) because of a name-matching error in the build script. They now show Restaurante Marabierta and Restaurante Vista al Mar as intended; the owner's first answers to these two rows are not used.
