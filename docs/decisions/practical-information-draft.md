## Taxi

Casa de la Familia is in Mijas, and Mijas council lists Radio Taxi Mijas as the local taxi service. Uber also operates in Mijas and Fuengirola, where local licensed drivers may take UberX requests. [12] Owner rule: guests going to bars use a taxi or Uber.

| Company | Phone | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Radio Taxi Mijas | 952 47 82 88 or 952 47 65 93 | Listed by Mijas council; web taximijas.es. The company's site also takes WhatsApp orders on 952 47 65 93 and its profile says it works 24 hours. [10][11] |
| Radio Taxi de Fuengirola | 952 47 10 00 | For trips starting in Fuengirola. Listed by Fuengirola's tourist information; the company says 24 hours and phone or WhatsApp on the same number. [13] |

## Train (Cercanías C1)

There is no station in Las Lagunas. Local line **C1** runs along the coast between **Fuengirola** and **Málaga Centro-Alameda**, calling at Málaga Airport and Málaga María Zambrano (the main station for long-distance trains). [14][15]

| Station | Drive from the house | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fuengirola (end of the line) | 13 min | Accessible; ticket machine and ticket office (Renfe). [16] [Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=10693301050779879544) |
| Los Boliches | 14 min | [Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=14875693478072807986) |
| Torreblanca | 15 min | [Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=4746835991639619492) |
| Carvajal | 18 min | [Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=10775807532647072485) |
| Benalmádena-Arroyo de la Miel | 24 min | Station for Arroyo de la Miel, Benalmádena. [Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=4786672058873118106) |
| Málaga Airport | 27 min by car | [Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=13448989070475443565) |

- **How often:** in the published timetable, trains leave Fuengirola every 20 minutes for most of the day (first about 06:10, last about 00:20), with some extra early trains on weekdays only. [15]
- **Journey times** from Fuengirola in that timetable: Málaga Airport about 34 minutes, Málaga María Zambrano about 43 minutes, Málaga Centro-Alameda about 46 minutes. [15]
- Timetables change; check Renfe's Cercanías Málaga page or its real-time service before travelling. [14]
- Parking at the stations, fares and payment methods are not checked yet.
