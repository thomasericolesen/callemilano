## Method

- Identity, address, website and position come from Google Places API (New), read 2026-11-02 (text searches for the named places, and nearby searches from the project origin: restaurants, cafés and bars within 1.5 km; parks and family attractions within 5-8 km). Maps establishes identity and position only.
- Walking and driving times from Google Routes API, 2026-11-02, from the project origin (36.5356875, -4.6626875). Walking = Google's walking route (it says nothing about pavements, crossings or whether the route suits children). Driving = Saturday 2026-11-07 10:00, predicted traffic.
- Nothing about menus, prices, opening hours, age limits or family suitability has been checked yet. Those need the venue's own or official sources when a record is made.
- Left out of the sweep results: shops, car parks, a dog park, a private summer camp, a birthday-party room, a dance school and a grave site.

## Observations

- **Truly walkable (under about 20 minutes):** three eateries (Mixs Lounge, Singh Doner Kebab, Restaurante Cerros del Águila) and, at 13-18 minutes, El Gran Parque de Mijas and the five buggy/quad operators on Calle la Higuera / El Membrillo. Equestrian Centre, Peña Al Tranco and the Camino Viejo de Coín restaurants are 16-21 minutes on foot.
- **A short drive (6-12 min):** JumpJoy, bowling, laser tag and play centres in Las Lagunas; the Miramar cluster (cinema, bowling, karting, VR) and BIOPARC/Alfil cinema in Fuengirola; Aquamijas (seasonal).
- **Aqualand Torremolinos** is 24 minutes by car: inside the 30-minute area but not near the house.
- **Buggy and quad tours** need age, licence and safety rules from each operator before any family rating.
