# Guest needs analysis

**Audience assumption:** Guests staying at Casa de la Familia in Cerros del Águila, Las Lagunas de Mijas. The guide should help them decide what to do, whether it fits their party and available time, and what they need to plan before leaving.

This prioritizes trip decisions over exhaustive place cataloguing. “Missing data” distinguishes (a) information the schema does not capture cleanly and (b) answers not yet established in the five pilot drafts. The pilots remain `needs_fact_check`; `docs/project-state.md` and `docs/pilot-review.md` report that driving times are null, duration is missing where duration tags are used, and access/facility and suitability values remain unresolved. These drafts do not establish destination facts.

“Unnecessary” means unnecessary for answering that particular guest question, not necessarily unnecessary for validation, editorial workflow, SEO, or rights management.

## Highest-priority information gaps

Before expanding the guide, close the gaps that most affect a guest’s go/no-go decision:

1. Verify destination identity, record scope, and representative point.
2. Provide dated drive estimates and realistic visit durations.
3. Verify current hours, booking rules, core prices, and material seasonal limitations.
4. Resolve family suitability, formal restrictions, physical effort, access, and parking from claim-specific evidence.
5. Explain total likely family cost and the practical facilities available on site versus nearby.

The schema is strongest at storing editorial, evidence, and lifecycle metadata. The most notable guest-facing gaps are total family cost, specific restrictions, stroller access, weather exposure, dietary/child food needs, and route details. These should first be tested as concise editorial content; add structured fields only if repeated guest decisions depend on them and the editor-of-record approves a schema change.

## Operational data that is not itself a guest answer

Fields such as stable `id`, `schema_version`, `status`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `review_decision`, `redirects`, `external_ids`, evidence checker identity, image-rights records, and validation outcomes support reliable production and maintenance. They are generally not guest-facing answers. Keep them in canonical records and operational systems, but do not let them displace concise answers about fit, time, cost, access, and current conditions.
