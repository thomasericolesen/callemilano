# Beach feedback survey (Facebook)

**Status:** planned 2026-10-05. Owner will post in Facebook groups and paste replies into a Claude session for sorting per beach.

**Why:** The beach candidates in `launch-candidates.md` were found by Codex from official sources. None has first-hand family experience behind it, and Reddit/TripAdvisor/Google reviews could not be retrieved by the tools. Replies are Layer 3 guest observations under `knowledge-model.md`: dated signals, not verified facts.

## Post drafts (Danish)

### Short

> Hej alle 👋
>
> Vi har hus i Cerros del Águila (Las Lagunas de Mijas) og får jævnligt besøg af familie med små børn. Hvilke strande mellem La Cala og Benalmádena vil I anbefale til børn under 6 år, og hvorfor?
>
> Vi er især interesserede i: hvor hurtigt det bliver dybt, bølger, sand eller sten, toiletter og skygge, parkering tæt på, og en chiringuito med børnevenlig mad.
>
> Skriv gerne, hvornår I var der, og hvor gamle børnene var. Tak på forhånd! ☀️

### With named beaches

> Hej alle 👋
>
> Vi har hus i Cerros del Águila (Las Lagunas de Mijas) og er ved at lave en lille guide til familie og gæster med små børn. Vi vil gerne høre jeres egne erfaringer, ikke det turistbrochurerne skriver.
>
> Har I været med børn under 6 år på nogle af disse strande? La Cala · El Bombo · Las Doradas / Calahonda · El Chaparral · strandene i Fuengirola og Los Boliches · Benalmádena
>
> Hvad virkede, og hvad virkede ikke? Fx hvor hurtigt det blev dybt, bølger, sand, toiletter, skygge, parkering og om der var en god chiringuito til børn.
>
> Skriv gerne hvornår (måned/år) og børnenes alder. Kender I en bedre strand, der ikke står på listen, hører vi også gerne om den. Tak! ☀️

### English (for British/expat groups)

> Hi all 👋
>
> Hoping some of you with little ones can help! We have a family house up in Cerros del Águila (Las Lagunas de Mijas), and we often have children and grandchildren visiting. We're putting together a little guide for them, just for family and friends, nothing commercial.
>
> We'd love to hear your own experiences rather than the glossy brochure version. Have you taken under-5s to any of these, and how did you get on? La Cala · El Bombo · Las Doradas / Calahonda · El Chaparral · Fuengirola and Los Boliches · Benalmádena
>
> The sort of things that make or break a day out with toddlers: does it shelve off quickly, are there waves, sand or pebbles, loos nearby, any shade, easy parking, and is there a chiringuito that's happy to feed fussy little ones?
>
> If you can mention roughly when you went and how old the children were, that would be brilliant. And if there's a hidden gem we've missed, we'd love to hear about that too!
>
> Thanks so much in advance ☀️

## Processing replies

For each reply record: beach, date of visit, children's ages, and statements on depth, waves, sand, toilets, shade, parking, lifeguard, chiringuito. Keep replies attributed and dated; do not copy them into place records as facts. Lifeguard season, flags and water quality must still come from the municipality.
