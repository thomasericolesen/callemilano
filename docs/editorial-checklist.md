# Destination editorial checklist

**Audience:** Guests staying at Casa de la Familia, Cerros del Águila. Apply this checklist to every destination. An unchecked required item means **hold publication**. Record sources, check dates, and the reviewer for material facts.

## Required before publication

- [ ] **Identity and scope:** Confirm the distinct place, public name, category, what the guest visits, and a named visitor point. Check for duplicates; town or area pages must state their scope.
- [ ] **A workable plan:** Give usable directions and a checked, dated, approximate one-way drive time from Casa de la Familia to that same visitor point. Give a sourced typical visit duration, qualified if it is an estimate.
- [ ] **Can guests go, and when?** Verify current opening status and applicable schedule, or clearly link to the responsible official live source. Check material seasonality and restrictions.
- [ ] **Will it work for this party?** Explain evidence-based party suitability and physical effort. Check formal age, height, supervision, or participation rules where relevant. State material access limitations; do not imply suitability where unknown.
- [ ] **Can they plan the cost and booking?** For paid destinations, show a checked price and its basis or link directly to current official pricing. Identify known required extra costs. State whether booking is required and provide the official route when applicable.
- [ ] **Evidence and accuracy:** Support each material factual claim with an appropriate source, URL, check date, and checker. Prefer official venue, government, tourism, transport, park, or heritage sources. Keep conflicts and material unknowns visible; never guess.
- [ ] **Guest-ready copy:** Describe the core experience clearly and distinguish facts from editorial advice. Confirm approved categories and labels, valid record data, unique identity/slug, and required validation. Get editor-of-record approval; drafts and failed validation are not publishable.

## Recommended before publication

- [ ] Verify parking, arrival restrictions, cost, and walk to the entrance; distinguish venue parking from nearby options.
- [ ] Check toilets, food, public transport/last mile, and dog rules when they could affect the visit. Say whether facilities are on-site or nearby; disclose unknowns.
- [ ] Add a concise, factual reason to choose this destination and useful activity/search wording. Link to an official contact route for unresolved practical questions when available.
- [ ] For restaurants, verify current operation, location, official menu/prices, booking, and relevant food or outside-food policies. For beaches and nature routes, verify the exact access point, route/segment status, effort, seasonal conditions, and material facilities. For activity venues and excursions, verify date-specific operation, age/height rules, booking, duration, access, and extra costs. For towns/cities, define the visitor area and arrival point; do not imply one pin covers the whole place.

## Optional after publication

- [ ] Add rights-cleared photos with accurate captions and useful alt text.
- [ ] Add brief background, nearby related places, or a multi-stop itinerary only when it improves a guest decision and travel/duration details are checked.
- [ ] Add same-day traffic, weather, or ticket availability only when it can be kept current; otherwise link to the responsible live source.
- [ ] Assign an owner and next review date. Recheck volatile details—hours, prices, booking, access, parking, transport, and facilities—at the approved interval, and route corrections for review.

**Publication gate:** If identity, visitor point, travel/time commitment, material opening, cost, participation, or access information is unresolved in a way that could waste the trip or make the destination unsuitable, hold the recommendation. Disclose non-blocking unknowns and provide a reliable next step. Images, extended background, and itineraries never substitute for required facts.
