# Schema gap analysis: guest decisions

**Scope:** Review of `docs/user-needs-analysis.md` against `docs/place-schema.md` version 1.0.0. This is analysis only; the schema is not modified.

Priority reflects how directly a missing or unclear answer can change a guest’s go/no-go choice or essential outing plan. **Critical** affects feasibility, cost, access, or a time-sensitive commitment. **Important** materially improves selection or preparation. **Nice to have** improves comparison or fallback planning but is less likely to determine the primary outing.

“Missing fields” means information not represented cleanly by a dedicated schema field. Existing fields may still carry it in prose or evidence. “Redundant fields” identifies actual overlap for that need; fields that are simply irrelevant are not called redundant.

## Findings for the 20 guest needs

| # | Priority and guest need | Supporting schema fields | Missing fields or distinctions | Redundant fields | Proposed changes |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Important — Is this a good choice for our group and day?** | `age_groups`, `family_score`, `energy_level`, `category`, `tags`, descriptions | No structured rationale/limitation per audience score; family fit and energy criteria remain editorial. | `age_groups` and `family_score` overlap if both are treated as simple age suitability; energy and duration can also be confused. | Keep age groups as eligible audience and scores as degree of fit; add concise score rationale/limitation guidance, not another rating field. Require human review and expose key caveats in guest copy. |
| 2 | **Critical — How long should we spend there?** | `visit_information.typical_duration`, duration tags, descriptions | Duration scope/assumptions are not structured; no explicit estimate confidence or basis. | `Half day`/`Full day` tags duplicate `typical_duration` as a time classification. | Make duration the source of truth; derive tags from it or omit tags. Define range, on-site scope, and whether estimate is official or editorial. |
| 3 | **Critical — How long is the drive, and where should we navigate?** | `driving_time_from_casa_de_la_familia`, `coordinates`, `name` | Route method and destination-point identity may be underspecified; no guest-facing route link is required. | Route object's `destination` can duplicate `coordinates.label`; `origin` repeats the fixed project origin on every record. | Keep one canonical destination label/reference and ensure it resolves to the coordinates. Consider a route URL/provider field if reproducibility requires it; keep the fixed origin in shared project config rather than duplicating if implementation permits. |
| 4 | **Important — When is the best time of year or day?** | `seasonality`, `visit_information.schedule`, validity dates, evidence | No explicit distinction encoded between suitability season and operating season; no time-of-day recommendation or weather basis. | `seasonality` and operating schedule overlap only if used to mean the same thing; otherwise they are complementary. | Define seasonality as visit suitability and schedule as operating availability. Put time-of-day advice in sourced prose only when it materially helps. Avoid adding a time-of-day field until repeated need is demonstrated. |
| 5 | **Critical — Will it be open on our chosen day?** | `visit_information.schedule`, validity dates, `links.visitor_information`, evidence | No structured exception/holiday calendar or “last checked for opening” date independent of generic evidence. | Schedule validity dates and evidence `checked_at` partially overlap as freshness indicators. | Use evidence `checked_at` as the source date and schedule validity for dates the schedule applies. Add a schedule-specific checked date only if the validator/display cannot reliably link evidence to the schedule. |
| 6 | **Critical — What will the outing cost our family?** | `cost_level`, `visit_information.cost_basis`, `cost_checked_at`, `parking.cost`, booking | No adult/child price breakdown, family ticket, party-size total, transport/meal extras, or price currency/unit detail beyond euro convention. | `cost_level` and `Free` tag overlap; `cost_checked_at` overlaps evidence date. | Keep `cost_level` as a coarse comparison aid. Consider an optional structured price list with ticket type, amount, currency, conditions, and checked date; calculate party totals only for a known party composition. Do not imply a universal family total. Derive or drop `Free` tag. |
| 7 | **Critical — Do we need to book, and how far ahead?** | `booking_required`, `visit_information.booking_lead_time`, `links.booking`, validity dates, evidence | No booking window, date-specific availability, slot capacity, or cancellation terms. | `booking_required` overlaps booking link only if the link is used as a proxy for whether booking is required; it should not be. | Keep policy separate from link. Add availability/cancellation data only if it can be maintained reliably; otherwise link to live operator booking and state that availability changes there. |
| 8 | **Critical — Can everyone manage the route, including stroller/wheelchair users?** | `accessibility.mobility.route`, `wheelchair_route`, facilities, `wheelchair_friendly`, accessible toilets | Stroller route/surface suitability is absent; gradients, distances, step-free route details and entrance-specific variation may be buried in free text. | `wheelchair_friendly` duplicates a summary of detailed mobility data; `accessibility.overall` may duplicate mobility and sensory details. | Define `wheelchair_friendly` as a derived display value and validate it. Remove or narrow `overall` if it merely restates detail. Add stroller-specific route value only if consistently verifiable; otherwise standardize a concise mobility route description. |
| 9 | **Critical — Where can we park, what does it cost, and how far is the walk?** | `parking.availability`, location, cost, restrictions, walk distance, source/date; coordinates | No capacity, height/vehicle limits as typed values, payment method, or walk route to the selected entrance. | `parking.source`/`checked_at` overlap the evidence ledger for the same parking claim. | Keep parking-specific source/date only if needed for convenience; otherwise make evidence canonical. Add restriction prose or a structured vehicle restriction only if common enough to maintain. Ensure `walk_distance` names its endpoint. |
| 10 | **Important — Are toilets and food available where we will be?** | `toilets_available`, `food_available`, accessible toilets, links | No facility location/scope, operating schedule, food type, or distance for nearby provision. | `toilets_available` overlaps `accessibility.accessible_toilets` only if the latter is interpreted as a second general toilet-availability field; accessible provision is a distinct attribute. | Keep availability and accessibility distinct. Specify on-site versus nearby and location in prose; consider a location/scope subfield if filtering by it becomes a demonstrated need. |
| 11 | **Important — Can we get there without driving?** | `transport_options`, `links.transport`, coordinates, drive time | No usable service schedule, transfer count, fare, journey duration, or last-mile effort in a consistent structure. | `transport_options` source/date and evidence ledger overlap if both independently duplicate the same claims. | Keep options concise and dated; link to live operator information for schedules/fare. Add journey duration/fare only if maintained and directly useful; evidence remains authoritative. |
| 12 | **Important — Can we bring our dog, and under what conditions?** | `dog_friendly`, evidence, official link | Enum lacks policy details such as leash, area, season, or assistance-animal distinction. | `dog_friendly` summary may duplicate detailed dog policy if prose is added. | Keep enum as a quick filter and use one concise policy note for material conditions; avoid adding many rarely populated subfields. |
| 13 | **Important — What exactly is this place and what will we experience?** | `name`, `category`, coordinate purpose/label, geography fields, descriptions, Maps URL | No explicit `place_scope` definition or visitor-facing explanation of what the selected pin represents. | `coordinates.label`, driving `destination`, and name can repeat the same point/place name; several geography fields are administrative rather than experience-defining. | Add a controlled/free-text scope field only if editorial rules cannot ensure clear descriptions. Otherwise require descriptions to name the represented site and point; reuse one canonical point label in coordinates and routing. |
| 14 | **Critical — Are there age, height, supervision, or other restrictions?** | `age_groups`, `family_score`, `visit_information`, descriptions, evidence | No dedicated structured restrictions field for minimum age/height, supervision, activity restrictions, or who each restriction applies to. | Age suitability and formal restriction overlap if `age_groups` is mistaken for formal entry rules. | Keep audience suitability separate. Add an optional structured `restrictions` list with type, affected activity/audience, rule, source, and checked date; this is high-value for activity venues. |
| 15 | **Important — Will weather or seasonal conditions affect comfort or activities?** | `seasonality`, schedule, descriptions, evidence | No fields for shade, shelter, exposure, weather-dependent operations, water conditions, or heat-related practical limits. | Seasonality overlaps schedule only if season labels are used to mean opening periods. | Keep general seasonal suitability and opening separate. Start with sourced prose for site-specific exposure; consider a small optional outdoor-conditions field only if recurring needs justify maintenance. |
| 16 | **Important — Can we eat there and meet children’s/dietary needs?** | `food_available`, official link, visit information, descriptions | No menu, child options, allergens/dietary options, meal timing, outside-food policy, or reservation details. | `food_available` and restaurant/food tags may duplicate the existence of food without adding useful detail. | Treat `food_available` as presence only. Use official menu/dietary links; consider structured dietary fields only where operators publish stable data and guests need filtering. Put outside-food policy in sourced prose if material. |
| 17 | **Important — What practical route constraints or extra travel costs apply?** | Driving time, coordinates, parking, transport, long description | No toll/ferry cost, road/vehicle restriction, access-road condition, route choice, or last-mile route guidance. | Route destination and coordinate label may duplicate; `transport_options` can overlap driving-route detail if modes are mixed. | Keep point and route destination aligned. Add an optional `route_notes` field only for verified material constraints, not turn-by-turn directions; link navigation/transport providers for live routing. |
| 18 | **Nice to have — What is the main reason to choose it over nearby options?** | Descriptions, category, tags, related places, images | No structured “distinctive experience” or comparison basis; this is largely editorial. | Category and tags can repeat the same classification without explaining the experience; short and long descriptions may duplicate each other. | Do not add a field. Give `description_short` a clear reason-to-visit role and `description_long` practical detail; curate related places only when comparison helps a guest choose. |
| 19 | **Important — How current and reliable are the practical details?** | Evidence dates, `last_verified`, `next_review_at`, issues, links | No clear field-level guest-facing freshness summary; `last_verified` semantics are unresolved. | `last_verified`, evidence `checked_at`, and `next_review_at` overlap as date/status signals but serve different purposes if defined correctly. | Keep evidence dates as source-level truth, `last_verified` as a clearly defined record-level check, and `next_review_at` internal. Generate a simple guest-facing “hours/price checked” label from relevant evidence instead of storing another date. |
| 20 | **Nice to have — What nearby or rainy-day alternative can we choose?** | Related places, tags, seasonality, coordinates, driving time | No alternative rationale, weather suitability, distance, or transit/drive time attached to a relationship. | `nearby` relationship and separate coordinates can duplicate proximity; duration tags overlap the duration field. | Extend related-place display/derived catalog with distance/time and a short reason when verified; do not add duplicate per-record prose or a new alternative field unless recommendations become a core product feature. |

## Highest-value schema work to consider

1. **Price detail for party decisions:** optional age-banded prices and conditions, with dates and a clearly defined basis. A total should be calculated for a stated party composition, not stored as a generic family price.
2. **Formal restrictions:** a structured, source-backed place for age/height, supervision, and activity-specific rules, distinct from editorial suitability ratings.
3. **Mobility detail:** clarify the derived wheelchair summary and capture stroller suitability or route detail only if reliably verifiable.
4. **Place scope and navigation point:** ensure the record tells a guest what the listing represents and routes to that exact visitor point.
5. **Field freshness:** resolve `last_verified` and map volatile claim dates to guest-visible facts; prefer generated summaries over duplicate stored timestamps.

Do not add fields solely to make every fact structured. For facilities, weather caveats, dietary details, and route notes, first test whether a concise, sourced description plus a link answers the guest’s decision reliably. Any schema change remains subject to editor-of-record approval and the project’s change-log process.
